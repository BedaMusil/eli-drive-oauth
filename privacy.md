# Privacy Policy

ELI Drive is a private application intended solely for personal use by its owner.

The application may access files and folders in the owner's Google Drive after explicit authorization through Google OAuth.

Google Drive data is used only to allow the owner's AI tools and assistants to read, process, create, and manage files requested by the owner.

Data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

Files may be processed or temporarily stored on a private server controlled by the application owner.

Access to Google Drive can be revoked at any time through the owner's Google Account security settings.

For questions regarding this application, contact the application owner through the support email configured in Google Cloud.
